How Auto-Save & Persistence Works

Layer 1: Instant Local Browser Storage (0ms Delay)

Every time you type a number, toggle a bill's PAID status, switch themes, or click outside an unlocked row, your entire 14-month ledger is saved immediately to your browser's localStorage. Refreshing or closing the tab will never lose your work.

Layer 2: Debounced Cloud Database Sync (650ms)

When connected to the cloud environment, your changes are automatically batched 650ms after your last keystroke and synced to your private Firestore document, keeping the status badge in the top bar updated in real time.

Click-Outside & Keyboard Commit

Double-click any row to unlock inline editing. As soon as you click anywhere outside that row (or press Enter / Esc), the row automatically locks back into its clean view and commits your changes.
